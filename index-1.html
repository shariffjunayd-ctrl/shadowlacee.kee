04 / A message from the founder

A vision.
An identity.

Nayd Ali · Founder & CEO

Shadowlace was born from a vision — to create something that goes beyond clothing and becomes a statement of identity.

From the beginning, I wanted Shadowlace to represent more than fashion. I wanted it to embody individuality, confidence and the quiet power of standing apart. Every piece, every detail and every decision reflects a belief that true style does not need to be loud to leave an impression.

Our philosophy is simple:

“We don't dress for trends. We dress for identity.”

As we continue to grow, our commitment remains the same: to create with intention, to stay true to our identity, and to build a brand that represents something meaningful.

Shadowlace is still writing its story, and this is only the beginning. What started as a vision is becoming a movement — one defined by creativity, ambition and the courage to be different.

Thank you to everyone who believes in the vision and continues to be part of our journey.

Shadowlace Wear — Shadows Run Nairobi.
— Nayd Ali, Founder & CEO
